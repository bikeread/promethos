# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

PromethOS is a shared library for agent-development workflows. Keep discussion
technical, respectful, and reviewable. Assume good intent, critique ideas
directly, and avoid personal attacks.

## Expected Behavior

- Share clear technical reasoning and concrete examples.
- Keep feedback specific to the code, docs, or workflow under discussion.
- Help contributors understand cross-harness tradeoffs instead of gatekeeping.
- Respect project scope, review boundaries, and repository rules.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks, dogpiling, or repeated bad-faith arguments
- Posting private information, secrets, or exploit details in public threads
- Deliberately disruptive spam or off-topic promotion

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that harms the project or
its contributors, and may restrict participation for repeated or serious
violations.

For general moderation concerns, contact the maintainers through the repository.
For sensitive cases, avoid posting personal details publicly and first request a
private follow-up channel.
